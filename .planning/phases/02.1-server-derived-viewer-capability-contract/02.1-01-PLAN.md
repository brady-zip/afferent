## Prohibitions

- Do not accept browser-supplied `userId`, `actorId`, `viewerActor`, `scopeId`, ownership, vote membership, admin state, or capability booleans; `sessionGeneration` is cache identity only.
- Do not call `ctx.auth` from the component, mutate/create/refresh actor rows from a query, scan actor/vote tables, or infer viewerHasVoted from `voteCount`.
